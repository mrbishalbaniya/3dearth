## Sketchfab

Models downloaded from [Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/) must follow each model’s license (typically CC BY). Credit the author here when you replace a shipped GLB with a Sketchfab asset.

Example:

```
F-22 Raptor FREE — NLM-Group — CC BY 4.0
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/f22-raptor-free-2a64abf0866a405c865466c7642ca689
```
